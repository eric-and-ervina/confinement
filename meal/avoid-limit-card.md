# 🚫 Avoid / Limit — Quick-Reference Card (fridge copy)

*Print this and stick it on the fridge. Full details live in [`meal-plan.md`](meal-plan.md) and [`recipes.md`](recipes.md).*

> ⚕️ **Not medical advice.** General guidance only — confirm specifics (medications, allergies, bleeding, supply worries) with her doctor, a lactation consultant, or a dietitian. Get medical help promptly for heavy bleeding, fever/chills or worsening pain, or a red, hot, painful breast with flu-like symptoms (possible mastitis).

---

## ❌ Avoid entirely

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| **High-mercury fish** — shark, swordfish, king mackerel (also marlin, bigeye tuna) | Mercury passes into breastmilk and can affect baby's developing nervous system |
| **Raw / cold food & drink** — salads, sushi, sashimi, iced drinks, cold water | Food-safety risk + against the confinement "everything warm" rule |
| **Alcohol** (beyond the small cooked amount in wine dishes) | Passes into breastmilk; does **not** boost supply. **Arak / strong spirits (30–40%) are a hard no** — not even "cooked off" |
| **Smoking / recreational drugs** | Harmful to baby via breastmilk |

---

## ⚠️ Limit

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Caffeine** (coffee, strong/black tea, cola, energy drinks) | Keep minimal while supply is establishing; caffeine can make baby fussy and jittery. Red date/longan tea is your everyday drink instead |
| **Wine dishes** (sesame oil chicken, yellow wine chicken, angkak chicken — the 🍶 marks in [`meal-plan.md`](meal-plan.md)) | Wine is for **flavour and tradition only — it does not boost milk supply.** Use low-alcohol confinement 米酒, simmer as long as the recipe allows (these cook 20–30 min under pressure, which does **not** fully drive the alcohol off), and **have her feed/pump just before the meal, or wait ~2 hours after it.** Most of these sit at lunch, so feed-first is the version you'll use; cooking them at dinner is the easy win |
| **Angkak 红曲米** | **Culinary amounts only — 1–2 tsp per pot**, as a colouring/flavouring — not a supplement (it contains monacolin K = lovastatin), and buy sealed, labelled packs only. **Pause it if lochia is still heavy or bleeding restarts** — it's 活血, blood-moving, the same rule as dang gui. **Not in week 1.** |
| **Dang gui (当归)** | Blood-moving herb — **pause it if lochia is still heavy or bleeding restarts.** Astragalus (黄芪) and red dates are safe throughout |
| **Batang 马鲛 (mackerel)** | Bigger ones accumulate more mercury. **Prefer small batang, and don't eat it more than ~once a week** — choose snapper/pomfret/seabass as the everyday fish instead |
| **Sugar / sweet dishes** (red date porridge, black sesame paste, oatmeal, red date-longan tea) | Most are "to taste" — **use as little sugar as possible**; a sweet dish most days is fine, a sweet-every-thing habit isn't |
| **Old ginger + longan (heaty 热气)** | With ginger + sesame oil + longan daily, some mothers run 热气 (sore throat, mouth ulcers). If that happens, ease off the ginger and longan a little |
| **Very rich / fried food** | Week 1 stays light — day 2's pork collar is trimmed lean with a light soy braise, not a rich stew. From week 2 a little richer is fine (the pork-belly braises on days 21 & 23). **Nothing deep-fried** |
| **Salt** (soy sauce, ikan bilis, 豆豉) | Soy sauce goes into most dishes and ikan bilis is salty. **If she had high BP or pre-eclampsia, keep it light** — BP still needs watching in the early postpartum weeks, and the calcium from ikan bilis isn't worth a salty pot |

---

> 🥜 **Peanut allergy in the family?** Swap the peanut & pork rib soup (days 8, 14, 22) for red date & chicken soup — same slot, no nuts.

---

## ✅ Do this instead

- **Drink ~2–3 L/day of warm liquids** — red date/longan tea (bedside thermos), soups, broths. Fluids support supply more than any single "booster" food.
- **Eat oatmeal most days** — oats + enough water is the evidence-backed pairing.
- **Feed / pump often** — more emptying of the breast = more supply. This beats any food.
- **Pick low-mercury fish** — threadfin 午鱼/马友 (ngoh her), snapper, pomfret, seabass, cod, salmon. (Batang 马鲛 is a cheaper substitute — but prefer small ones and max ~once a week.)
- **Keep everything warm** — every vegetable cooked, every drink warm.

---

*Green papaya fish soup and black sesame are lovely, nourishing traditional galactagogues — but their effect is anecdotal. Enjoy them; promise a bit less.*
