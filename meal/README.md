# 🍲 28-Day Confinement (坐月子) Meal Plan — Singapore

A complete home-cooking plan for a husband preparing 28 days of postpartum meals for his wife, using ingredients readily available in Singapore.

**Plan notes:**
- ✅ Pork is fine — but **no pork innards and no pig trotters**
- ✅ Breastfeeding — lactation-boosting foods emphasised (weeks 3–4)
- ✅ Simple home-style dishes — nothing that takes all day
- ⚠️ Delivery method TBD — plan works for both; see the C-section note below

---

## The 4 Golden Rules

1. **Everything warm.** No raw salads, no iced drinks, no cold water. All vegetables cooked. Plain water is replaced by warm red date/longan tea — keep a thermos on her bedside.
2. **Ginger + sesame oil + rice wine** are your base. Old ginger (老姜) goes into nearly every savoury dish.
3. **Small frequent meals.** 3 meals + 2 snacks/teas a day beats 3 big meals.
4. **No innards, no trotters** — swapped for pork ribs/collar/lean pork, chicken, fish, beef, eggs. Protein and collagen needs are fully covered without them.

## Week-by-Week Logic

| Week | Theme | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (D1–7) | 排毒 Gentle recovery | Expel lochia, rest digestion — light soups, congee, steamed fish |
| 2 (D8–14) | 收缩 Blood-building | Red dates, sesame oil chicken starts, more protein |
| 3 (D15–21) | 进补 Lactation boost ⭐ | Papaya fish soup 4–5×, oatmeal, herbal black chicken |
| 4 (D22–28) | 调理 Strengthening | Wider variety, richer soups, preparing to re-enter normal life |

> 💡 **If C-section:** add 生鱼 (snakehead) soup 4–5× in weeks 1–2 for wound healing, keep ginger slightly milder in the first week, and hold off on strong tonics (dang gui, ginseng) until day 10+.

## Quick answers

- **What should I cook first?** Start with the Week 1 meals — they're the gentlest and easiest (porridge, steamed fish, light soups).
- **What boosts milk supply?** The evidence-based trio: oatmeal most days, ~2–3 L/day of warm fluids, and frequent feeding/pumping. Traditional boosters (green papaya fish soup, black sesame) are nourishing additions, but their galactagogue effect is anecdotal.
- **What if she gets bored?** Swap any fish for another fish, any leafy green for another leafy green — the structure stays the same.
- **Alcohol in rice wine?** Wine doesn't boost milk supply — it's for flavour and tradition — and alcohol passes into breastmilk. Long simmers (60+ min) reduce alcohol to trace levels. Short simmer (20–30 min) retains ~30%, so for shorter dishes use a low-alcohol confinement-brewed 米酒, or simmer longer — see the ⚠️ notes in recipes.md. Practical rule: cook wine dishes in the evening, or plan feeds ~2 hours after eating them.
