# Ervina's Keepers — dishes to cook again after confinement

Saved at Ervina's request on **10 Nov 2026 (Day 16)** — the dishes she liked most during confinement and wants to cook again after it ends.

Methods below are **the versions actually cooked and eaten in this house**, which in two cases (lean pork, pork collar) differ from the book text in [`recipes.md`](recipes.md) — those are written out in full. The other three point at their book recipes, with the as-cooked notes that matter.

---

## 1. Sesame Oil Chicken (麻油鸡) — book recipe 1

*As validated on Day 12 (6 Nov): "great, unlike Day 8." The fix that made it: wine as a 2 tsp background splash, never a braise.*

- 500 g chicken (bone-in thighs or kampung chicken)
- 3 tbsp roasted sesame oil · 8–10 slices old ginger
- 2 tsp huadiao (花雕) · ~100 ml water · 1 tsp brown sugar (optional)

1. Pat the chicken dry. Sesame oil in the pan on **medium-low**, fry the ginger until golden at the edges — by colour, ~2–3 min on our stove.
2. Sear the chicken golden on all sides (two batches if crowded).
3. Splash the 2 tsp huadiao around the hot pan's edge; scrape up the browned bits as it sizzles.
4. Add ~100 ml water (just enough to keep the pan moist), lid on, **gentlest simmer 15–20 min**, turning once. Start checking at 15 min.
5. Plate and cover when done — do not hold it in the pan (Day 12's toughness came from a long hold).

Full text: recipes.md #1.

## 2. Sesame-Oil Pork Slices with Ginger (麻油猪肉片) — book recipe 40

- 200 g pork collar or belly, thinly sliced
- 1.5 tbsp sesame oil · 8 slices ginger
- 1 tbsp rice wine · 1 tsp light soy sauce · 100 ml water

1. Sesame oil in the pan on **medium**, fry ginger until golden.
2. Add the pork, golden on both sides.
3. Wine + water + soy in; simmer **10–12 min** (check at 8 — the pan reduces fast) until tender.

Full text: recipes.md #40.

## 3. Angkak Chicken (红曲米鸡) — book recipe 69, as cooked Day 14

*The version Ervina ate and liked was the pressure-cooker run on 8 Nov (Day 14 lunch), with frozen IQF thighs — not the book's pan-first version.*

- ~600–650 g chicken thighs, chopped (frozen IQF works)
- 1–2 tsp angkak （红曲米）, mashed to a paste with ~30 ml rice wine （米酒/黄酒， **not arak**)
- 2 tbsp roasted sesame oil · 8–10 slices old ginger · ~150 ml water
- 1 tsp light soy · 1 tsp brown sugar · a splash of vinegar · 50 g black fungus or mushrooms (optional)

1. Fry the ginger in the sesame oil in the cooker's **inner pot on the gas ring** until golden; sear the chicken.
2. Stir in the angkak paste; toss until every piece is coated red.
3. Add water, fungus/mushrooms, soy, sugar, vinegar. Wipe the pot dry outside, seat it in the cooker.
4. **鸡鸭肉 / soft setting ~42 min** (the frozen-thigh run). Open, and reduce with 开盖收汁 if the sauce is loose.

Full text (pan version + cautions on angkak dosing): recipes.md #69.

## 4. Pan-Braised Lean Pork (焖瘦肉) — as cooked Days 15 & 16, NOT the book method

*Book recipe 44 sends this to the pressure cooker. What Ervina actually liked — twice — is Eric's pan version. This is that version, for 250 g; the Day 16 lunch used the spare 250 g of slices exactly this way.*

- 250 g lean pork, sliced
- 1 tbsp sesame oil · 6 slices ginger
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce · 1 tsp brown sugar · **200 ml water**

1. Pat the pork dry.
2. Pan on **medium**: sesame oil + ginger, fry until golden (2–3 min).
3. Sear the pork slices, ~1 min per side.
4. Add soy, sugar and the 200 ml water; scrape the pan bottom.
5. Lid on, **gentle simmer 10–12 min**, until the liquid reduces to a light glaze coating the slices.
6. Serve hot over rice.

## 5. Braised Pork Collar with Peanuts (花生焖猪颈肉) — as cooked Day 16 dinner

*Born 10 Nov 2026: book recipe 45 wanted chestnuts, none could be found (wet market, FairPrice, Giant all out) — raw peanuts replaced them and the dish was a hit. Chunks, not the book's slices; water corrected by Eric mid-cook ("braise or soup?") from a doubled 800 ml to 500 ml.*

- 500 g pork collar, cut in 3–4 cm chunks （梅花肉切块）
- **90–100 g raw peanuts** (not roasted/salted) — the cook used the old pack's last 90 g
- 1 tbsp sesame oil · 12 slices ginger
- 3 tbsp light soy sauce · 2 tsp brown sugar · **500 ml water** (about ⅔ up the meat)

1. Pat the pork dry. Sesame oil + ginger in the cooker's **inner pot on the gas ring**, medium, until golden.
2. Sear the chunks until coloured on most sides (two batches if crowded).
3. Add the **peanuts now — they need the full pressure time** (unlike chestnuts, which go in after), then the water, soy and sugar. Scrape the pot bottom.
4. Wipe the pot dry outside, seat it in the cooker, lid locked. **肉排骨， 口感适中** — the mode's default on our machine is **45 min**, which suits chunks. (Book time for slices was ~30 min.)
5. Vent, open, **开盖收汁 10–12 min** to a glossy, coating sauce. Taste partway — the soy is the only salt; stop the reduce when it tastes right.
6. Ladling note: if you ever need to take liquid out before cooking, what floats off is mostly the sesame oil — accepted, no top-up needed; collar + peanuts give richness to spare.

Served Day 16 with stir-fried cabbage (¼ head shredded — which cooked down to very little; use ⅓–½ head when cabbage is the vegetable) and reheated rice.
