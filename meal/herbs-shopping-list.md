# 🌿 Herbs Shopping Checklist — Regular Meal Plan

**For the regular food plan only** ([`meal-plan.md`](meal-plan.md)). The TCM track's full formulas (生化汤, 四物汤, 杜仲…) live in [`../tcm/herb-guide.md`](../tcm/herb-guide.md) and are only bought if a TCM physician prescribes them.

## The 6 herbs to buy

| # | Herb | Buy | Used for (in the plan) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **红枣 Red dates** | **~2 kg** (buy 1 kg, restock once) | Heaviest-used item: daily red date / red date + longan tea (bedside thermos 2× daily), red-date porridge breakfasts (days 3, 11, 21, 27), 8 pieces per herbal soup pot (days 17, 19, 23, 24, 28), pork ribs w/ lotus root + red dates (day 20) |
| 2 | **老姜 Old ginger** | **~2 kg** (a big hand up front, restock weekly) | Daily: ginger porridges/congee, steamed fish w/ ginger, ginger in stir-fries and soups, 2 slices per warm soy milk |
| 3 | **枸杞 Goji berries** | **~200 g** | Steamed chicken w/ goji (day 4), black chicken congee (day 16), ~1 tbsp per herbal soup pot |
| 4 | **龙眼肉 Longan (dried)** | **~200 g** | Daily red date + longan tea, 10 pieces per herbal soup pot |
| 5 | **当归 Dang gui** | **~100 g loose** | Herbal black chicken soup only — 12 g/pot × 5 pots (days 17, 19, 23, 24, 28) ≈ 60 g needed |
| 6 | **黄芪 Astragalus** | **~100 g loose** | Herbal black chicken soup only — 15 g/pot × 5 pots ≈ 75 g needed |

> Herbal soup pot = [recipe 3](recipes.md#3-herbal-black-chicken-soup-药材鸡汤) (DIY pack: 12 g dang gui + 15 g astragalus + 8 red dates + 10 longan + 1 tbsp goji). One pack = one pot = 1–2 servings; don't reuse.

## Buying tips (Singapore)

- Chinese medical halls (Eu Yan Sang, Ma Kuang, Hockhua, or your neighbourhood 药材店) will weigh loose herbs to these amounts. Tell them it's for **产后 / confinement** — they'll recognise everything.
- Ask for **无硫 / sulphur-free** where offered — better taste, safer.
- Or buy a pre-packed confinement herbal set and supplement red dates, ginger, goji, and longan separately.

## ⛔ Do not buy

- **麦芽 / 大麦芽 (barley malt)** — 回乳, suppresses milk. It hides in some "milk-regulating" teas that do the opposite of what you want.
- Anything from the TCM formulary (生化汤, 四物汤, 杜仲, 川芎…) unless her TCM physician prescribes it — the regular plan doesn't use them.

*Amounts are estimates based on the meal plan at HEAD — buy the loose herbs (5, 6) slightly above need, and restock red dates and ginger once mid-month.*
