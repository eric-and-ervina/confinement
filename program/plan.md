# 📅 The 12-Week Recovery Plan (+ beyond)

The full holistic week-by-week plan. It runs **alongside** the 28-day meal plan in [`../meal/meal-plan.md`](../meal/meal-plan.md) — that plan covers food for weeks 1–4; this one carries everything else and keeps going to the 12-week mark and past it.

**How to read it:** each week gives a **Body & movement**, **Appearance**, and **Mind & rest** focus. Food is handled by the meal plan unless something extra is noted. Do only what you have clearance and energy for — a "low week" where you do 20% of it is still a success.

> 🎯 **Her birth:** unmedicated vaginal delivery, **one stitch (minor perineal tear)**, no C-section. So the first job in weeks 1–2 is a **healed perineum** — perineal hygiene, sitz baths, a soft stool, and protecting the stitch. Full detail in [`perineal-care.md`](perineal-care.md). C-section notes below are fallback only.

> ⚕️ **Rule zero for the first 6 weeks:** gentle walking, breathing, pelvic-floor "wake-up", and rest only. Nothing more, no matter how good she feels — bleeding, leaking, or heaviness are the body's stop signals. See [`movement.md`](movement.md).

## Phase 1 — Restore (Weeks 0–6)

**Theme:** protect the healing. The body is closing a wound the size of a dinner plate (where the placenta was), **the perineal tear is knitting itself back together**, the hormones are falling fast, and sleep is broken. Almost all "appearance" change is *supposed* to be happening right now. Our job is to support it, not fight it.

> 🧻 **The perineal routine runs every day of Phase 1** — it is not a one-off: **warm peri-bottle rinse + pat dry + fresh pad after every toilet trip; a 10–15 min warm sitz bath 2–3× a day from day 2; keep ahead of the pain; short spells of sitting; and never, ever strain on the toilet.** The single most load-bearing rule is the **soft stool** — which is exactly why the [meal plan's](../meal/meal-plan.md) warm fruit, fibre greens and 2–3 L of warm fluids are non-negotiable for her in Week 1. Full detail in [`perineal-care.md`](perineal-care.md).

| Week | Body & movement | Appearance | Mind & rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Perineal care first:** peri-bottle rinse after every toilet trip, ice over the pad in the first 48 h, sitz bath from day 2, fresh pad every 2–4 h, roll out of bed · 5 slow belly breaths, several times a day · ankle pumps & circles every hour you're awake (clot prevention) · short walks around the house · rest lying down when possible | Warm showers, gentle moisturiser only. **Start daily broad-spectrum SPF 30–50** if she goes outdoors. Let the wound/discharge be, keep clean and dry. Nothing fancy on skin yet | Sleep whenever the baby sleeps — genuinely. Accept every offer of help. Watch for baby blues around day 3–5. No visitors who need entertaining |
| **2** | **Perineal routine continues** (rinse, sitz, pads, no straining) — days 3–5 are the sorest, so keep ahead of the pain · gentle pelvic tilts · heel slides · sit up by rolling to one side (protect the belly wall) · walks 5–10 min, 1–2× a day | Keep skin routine to cleanse–moisturise–SPF. Begin **breast/pumping care** (lanolin, keep dry). Hydrate — it helps skin and milk | The blues, if any, should be easing by day 10–14. If low mood is deepening instead, read [`mind-body.md`](mind-body.md) |
| **3** | Pelvic-floor "elevator" breathing (see [`movement.md`](movement.md)) · continue walks, build toward 10–15 min | **Hair-shedding prep** (it starts soon): gentle care, no tight styles. Skincare: add a gentle niacinamide or azelaic acid if skin is oily/pigmenting | Peak exhaustion + cluster feeding week. Rope in a night shift if you can (you take one feed with expressed milk, or settle the baby) |
| **4** | 360° breathing with pelvic-floor connection · posture reset after feeds (chest opener, neck release) · walks 15–20 min | **Melasma care is mostly sun-care:** hat + shade + reapply SPF. Don't buy whitening creams — see the warning in [`appearance.md`](appearance.md) | First "is this forever?" dip is normal. Talk to one trusted person. Keep a simple mood note (tracker) |
| **5** | Same gentle base, plus side-lying clamshells if comfortable · by now the **stitch has usually dissolved and the skin healed** — the deeper tissue still needs a few more weeks, so keep avoiding straining and long sitting ([`perineal-care.md`](perineal-care.md)) *(C-section, fallback: start gently massaging the scar only if the doctor says the wound is healed)* | **Stretch-mark care:** keep skin moisturised, massage in a centella/hyaluronic cream. Fresh marks are red now — that's the treatable stage | Baby's first growth spurt = more feeding, less sleep. Lower the bar on everything else |
| **6** | **Milestone: the 6-week check.** Ask specifically about the **perineal tear/scar**, pelvic floor + tummy gap (diastasis), and get clearance for exercise · keep walking | Bloods if not done: **iron/ferritin, vitamin D, thyroid** — these drive hair, energy and skin. Dental check if gums bleed | Review mood honestly with the doctor — PPD can appear any time in the first year, not just early |

> 🀄 **中文: 产后一个月是"养"出来的，不是"练"出来的。** 前六周以休息、温食、呼吸和散步为主 — 重训、跑步、卷腹都要等医生和物理治疗师放行。

## Phase 2 — Rebuild (Weeks 6–12, after clearance)

**Theme:** rebuild the deep core and pelvic floor first, then strength, then fitness. This is where hair falls and then regrows, stretch marks start fading, and *real* energy returns.

| Week | Body & movement | Appearance | Mind & rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| **7** | **After clearance only.** Begin core rehab: breathing with deep-core connection, dead bugs, glute bridges, bird-dog (see [`movement.md`](movement.md)) · walks 20–30 min | Add a **retinoid-free** evening routine: cleanse, treat (vitamin C in the morning), moisturise. If breastfeeding, hold off on retinal/tretinoin until she stops (or ask the doctor) | **Hair shedding often begins around now and peaks around months 4–5.** Warn her ahead of time so it doesn't frighten her — it regrows |
| **8** | Core rehab 3×/week · add posture strength: rows, chin tucks, upper-back work (it fixes the "mommy hunch") · cardio: brisk walking | **Scalp health:** wash when needed, gentle massage, wide-tooth comb, protein at meals (hair is made of protein). Check ferritin (above) | Start reclaiming one thing that's *hers* — a 20-min shower, a walk alone, a call with a friend |
| **9** | Core rehab + first light strength: bodyweight squats, hip hinges, wall push-ups, light dumbbells · 2–3×/week | **Stretch marks turn silver and soften** around now. Moisturise + keep the marks out of the sun (sun darkens them). Retinoids (post-breastfeeding) or dermatologist options exist | Sleep strategy review: are shifts actually working? Fix the worst night. Track mood weekly |
| **10** | Add: farmer carry (light), side plank from knees, standing hip abduction · keep progressing without pain/leaking | **Hands & feet:** foot may be a half size bigger — get properly fitting, supportive shoes; "mommy thumb" wrist pain → brace + ergonomics (see [`appearance.md`](appearance.md)) | Intimacy often resumes around now if she wants it — read the pelvic-health + dryness notes in [`mind-body.md`](mind-body.md) before assuming anything |
| **11** | Strength 2–3×/week + cardio 150 min/week (brisk walking, stationary bike, or swimming once cleared) · add single-leg balance | Skincare consistency beats intensity. **Recheck:** is the SPF routine actually happening? Reapply — there isn't a pigment cream that beats sun avoidance | Review the whole tracker. Celebrate non-scale wins: energy, walks taken, sleep nights, mood trend |
| **12** | Full rehab routine: core, strength, cardio. **Running/impact only after a women's-health physio screen** (no leaking, no heaviness, good single-leg strength) | Book a "reset" appointment if wanted: dermatologist for melasma/marks, dentist, GP for bloods/BP/thyroid | **The 3-month check-in:** how is her mood, sleep, pelvic health, and sense of self? Adjust the program, keep what works |

## Phase 3 — Return (Months 3–12)

**Theme:** permanent change is normal. Now the goal is strength, stamina, and looking well *for the long run* — not for a photo.

| Window | What to do | What to expect |
|---|---|---|
| **Months 3–4** | Progressive strength 3×/week (squats, hinges, push, pull, carry), cardio 150 min/week, keep the pelvic-floor routine forever | Hair shedding peaks then slows; new baby hairs appear at the hairline. Energy should be climbing |
| **Months 4–6** | Add heavier lifting, classes, sport — as her schedule and body allow. Keep core/pelvic work in the warm-up | Melasma fades as hormones settle; if she's stopped breastfeeding, retinoids/treatment can be added. Waist may settle at a new (often wider) normal — that's bones, not failure |
| **Months 7–9** | Maintain 3× strength + cardio. Strength training protects **bone density**, which matters after pregnancy and breastfeeding | Sleep improves as the baby sleeps longer; mood stabilises. Menstrual cycles may return (plan contraception — see [`mind-body.md`](mind-body.md)) |
| **Months 10–12** | Full return: run, lift, play, whatever she loves. Annual GP check: BP, iron, thyroid, vitamin D | Most women feel "back" — a stronger, differently-shaped version. Some stretch marks, a slightly different waist, and looser feet may stay. That's normal, not a problem to fix |

> 💡 **Weight, honestly:** most women lose ~5–6 kg at delivery, then roughly 0.5–1 kg per month in the early months while eating well and moving. **Never below ~1,800 kcal/day while breastfeeding** (milk needs ~330–400 extra kcal/day), no crash diets, no detoxes, no meal-skipping — they cut supply, hair, mood and energy. The [meal plan](../meal/meal-plan.md) is already built for this. Fat loss is a Phase 3 subject, gently and slowly.

## The daily rhythm (all phases)

A repeatable loop that takes under 30 minutes of "program" time, plus rest:

1. **Wake:** warm fluids, warm breakfast (meal plan), 5–10 slow breaths.
2. **Every toilet trip (Phase 1):** peri-bottle warm rinse → pat dry → fresh pad; sitz bath 2–3× a day.
3. **Mid-morning:** pelvic-floor + posture routine (5–10 min), a short walk.
4. **Feeds:** use each feed as a cue — chest opener + neck release after, sip water.
5. **Afternoon:** skin routine (cleanse–treat–moisturise), rest lying down if possible.
6. **Evening:** strength/core routine on lifting days, wind-down, SPF off, moisturise, bed early.
7. **Always:** accepted help, protected sleep, one thing that's hers.
