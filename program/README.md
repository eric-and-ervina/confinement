# 🌿 The After-Birth Recovery Program — 产后调理 (12 weeks & beyond)

A complete, holistic program for a new mother to **recover her health and her appearance after pregnancy** — the companion to the 28-day confinement meal plan in [`../meal/`](../meal/README.md).

The meal plan feeds her. **This program looks after everything else:** rest, movement, skin, hair, mood, medical follow-up, and the slow return to feeling like herself.

> ⚕️ **Read this first.** This is a well-researched, practical *self-care* program — not medical advice, and not a substitute for her doctor, midwife, or a women's-health physiotherapist. Recovery timelines depend on how she delivered (vaginal or C-section), complications (anaemia, high blood pressure, gestational diabetes, tearing, infection), how she's feeding, and her own body. **Get clearance from her OB/midwife before any exercise beyond gentle walking and breathing**, especially after a C-section or a severe tear. When in doubt, ask a professional — that is not weakness, it is good care.

---

## The whole idea in one line

**Recover, don't rebound.**

Pregnancy and birth change a body permanently and normally — bones shift, the belly wall and pelvic floor stretch, hormones fall, sleep disappears. The goal is not to "get your body back." It is to **heal thoroughly, rebuild strength, and look well** — which, done properly, is what actually makes a new mother feel and look like herself again. Crash diets, punishing workouts, and comparison to strangers on the internet do the opposite: they slow healing, hurt milk supply, and feed exhaustion.

## The timeline at a glance

| Phase | When | Theme | Headline moves |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Restore** | Weeks 0–6 (the confinement month + buffer) | Rest, heal, protect | Warm nourishing food, breathing, pelvic floor "wake-up", short walks, skin barrier, sleep, doctor reviews. **No exercise beyond gentle walking/breathing.** |
| **2 — Rebuild** | Weeks 6–12 (after the 6-week check) | Rebuild from the inside out | Core & pelvic-floor rehab, gentle strength, cardio, stretch marks & hair, mood check, GP/dental checks. |
| **3 — Return** | Months 3–12 | Return to full life, permanently changed | Progressive strength 3×/week, cardio 150 min/week, running/impact only after a physio screen, hormones settle and skin/hair improve on their own. |

> 💡 Most of the *appearance* side effects (hair shedding, melasma, linea nigra, swelling) peak in weeks 1–4 and then improve **on their own** over the following months as hormones settle. Phase 1 is about protecting that natural recovery, not interfering with it.

## 🚨 Emergency red flags — get medical help now

If any of these appear, do not wait for the 6-week check. Go to the doctor, or A&E:

| Symptom | Why it's urgent |
|---|---|
| **Heavy bleeding** — soaking a pad in an hour, or passing big clots | Postpartum haemorrhage |
| **Fever ≥ 38 °C, chills, foul-smelling discharge** | Infection (womb, wound, or urine) |
| **Calf pain, swelling, redness, warmth in one leg** | Possible deep-vein thrombosis (blood clot) — also chest pain, breathlessness, or coughing blood = **emergency, call 995** |
| **Severe headache, blurred vision, flashing lights, upper-abdominal pain, sudden swelling** | Possible pre-eclampsia/eclampsia after birth |
| **Wound not healing, opening, or angry red and hot** (C-section or tear) | Wound infection |
| **Breast red, hot, hard, wedge-shaped, with fever/flu feeling** | Mastitis — needs prompt treatment |
| **Thoughts of harming yourself or the baby, or of "disappearing"** | Postpartum crisis — seek help immediately (see [`mind-body.md`](mind-body.md)) |
| **Feeling faint, racing heart, extreme breathlessness, or bleeding that restarts** | Call the doctor right away |

## Quick answers

- **Where do I start?** With your wife, on day 1: warm meals from the [meal plan](../meal/meal-plan.md), 5 slow breaths a few times a day, ankle pumps, and as much rest as the newborn allows. That's Phase 1.
- **When can she exercise?** Gentle walking and breathing from day 1 (if comfortable). Everything else waits for the **6-week check** and her doctor's clearance — and for core work, ideally a women's-health physio screen.
- **Will her hair fall out?** It will, starting around month 3 and peaking around months 4–5 — and it regrows. It's a hormone crash, not a deficiency. See [`appearance.md`](appearance.md).
- **How long until she feels like herself?** Most women feel meaningfully better by 3 months and "back" — a new version of it — by 6–12 months. Faster is not better.
- **What if she's bottle-feeding?** Every part of this program still applies. Just skip the breastfeeding-specific notes.
- **What if it's a C-section?** Same program, gentler first 2 weeks: no lifting beyond baby + car seat, splint the wound when coughing/sneezing, and wound/scar care starts once the doctor says the wound is healed (see [`appearance.md`](appearance.md)).
