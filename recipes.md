# 👨‍🍳 Signature Recipes

Step-by-step recipes for the dishes that appear most often in the 28-day plan. All quantities serve 1–2 people (scale up as needed).

---

## 1. Sesame Oil Chicken (麻油鸡)

*Appears: days 8, 14, 15, 18, 22, 26*

**Ingredients**
- 500 g chicken (prefer 甘榜/camp chicken or bone-in thighs)
- 3 tbsp sesame oil (use roasted, not the pale cooking kind)
- 8–10 slices old ginger (老姜)
- 150 ml rice wine (米酒/黄酒) + water to cover
- 1 tbsp brown sugar (optional)

**Method**
1. Heat sesame oil in a wok over low–medium heat. Fry the ginger slices until golden and fragrant (5–7 min) — this is where the flavour lives.
2. Turn up heat, add chicken, sear until golden on all sides.
3. Pour in rice wine, then enough water to barely cover. Bring to a boil, reduce to a gentle simmer.
4. Simmer uncovered 25–30 min until tender and the wine smell has mellowed. Add sugar if you like.
5. Serve with rice or 面线.

**Trick:** simmer the wine at least 20–30 min after adding so the alcohol cooks off and only the fragrance remains.

> ⚠️ **Alcohol note (breastfeeding):** a 30-minute simmer still leaves ~30% of the alcohol. For peace of mind, simmer 60+ minutes, use low-alcohol confinement-brewed wine (米酒), or swap some of the wine for extra water/stock.

## 2. Green Papaya Fish Soup (青木瓜鱼汤) ⭐ Lactation Booster

*Appears: days 15, 16, 18, 21, 26*

**Ingredients**
- 400 g fish (snakehead 生鱼, threadfin 马鲛, or batang) — fillets + bones
- 1 semi-ripe green papaya, peeled, seeded, chunked
- 6–8 slices old ginger
- 1.5 L boiling water (this is the milky-broth secret)
- Salt to taste (light)

**Method**
1. Pat fish dry. Heat a little sesame oil in a pot, fry ginger until fragrant.
2. Add fish (bones first, then fillets) and fry until lightly golden.
3. Pour in **boiling** water — this instantly makes the broth milky white.
4. Add papaya chunks. Simmer 25–30 min until papaya is soft and translucent.
5. Season lightly, serve hot.

**Trick:** frying the fish + adding boiling water = the white colour. Never add cold water.

## 3. Herbal Black Chicken Soup (药材鸡汤)

*Appears: days 17, 19, 23, 24, 28*

**Ingredients**
- 1 black chicken (Silkie 乌鸡), chopped
- 1 confinement herbal pack (dang gui 当归, astragalus 黄芪, red dates 红枣, goji 枸杞, longan 龙眼) — or assemble your own
- 8 slices ginger
- 2 L water
- 1 tbsp rice wine (optional)

**Method**
1. Blanch the chicken in boiling water 2 min, drain (removes scum).
2. Rinse the dried herbs. Add everything to a pot or slow cooker.
3. Pour in 2 L water, bring to a boil, skim, then simmer 1.5–2 hours (or slow cooker 4 hours).
4. Season lightly. Drink the soup, eat the chicken.

**Shortcut:** buy pre-packed confinement herbal packs from Eu Yan Sang, Ma Kuang, or any Chinese medical hall — zero measuring needed.

## 4. Yellow Wine Chicken (黄酒鸡)

*Appears: days 12, 20, 25*

**Ingredients**
- 500 g chicken, chopped
- 250 ml yellow wine (黄酒) or red yeast rice wine (红糟酒)
- 8 slices ginger
- 2 tbsp sesame oil
- 50 g black fungus (soaked, optional)

**Method**
1. Fry ginger in sesame oil until golden. Add chicken, sear well.
2. Add black fungus, then pour in the yellow wine and a splash of water.
3. Simmer 20–30 min so the wine mellows. Serve hot over rice.

> ⚠️ **Alcohol note (breastfeeding):** simmer at least 60 minutes (or use low-alcohol confinement wine) if you want to keep the alcohol content minimal.

## 5. Ginger Millet Porridge (姜香小米粥)

*Breakfast staple, weeks 1–4*

**Ingredients**
- 100 g millet (小米) or rice
- 1 L water
- 5–6 slices ginger
- 6 red dates, pitted
- 1 egg (soft-boiled or poached, served on top)

**Method**
1. Rinse millet. Bring water + ginger + red dates to a boil.
2. Add millet, simmer 25–30 min stirring occasionally until thick and soft.
3. Top with an egg. Sweeten with a little brown sugar if she likes.

## 6. Red Date & Longan Tea (红枣龙眼茶)

*The bedside drink — make a big pot daily*

**Ingredients**
- 8 red dates, pitted, sliced
- 10 longan (龙眼) dried
- 2 slices ginger
- 1.5 L water
- Brown sugar to taste (optional)

**Method**
1. Simmer everything in a pot 30–40 min until the water turns amber and fragrant.
2. Strain into a thermos. Refill the bedside flask 2× daily. Warm, never cold.

## 7. Peanut & Pork Rib Soup (花生排骨汤)

*Appears: days 8, 14, 22*

**Ingredients**
- 400 g pork ribs
- 100 g raw peanuts (skins on)
- 6 red dates
- 8 slices ginger
- 2 L water

**Method**
1. Blanch ribs 2 min, drain.
2. Add all ingredients, bring to boil, skim, simmer 1.5 hours.
3. Season lightly. Great for strength and (with the red dates) blood-building.

## 8. Steamed Fish with Ginger (姜丝蒸鱼)

*Appears regularly in weeks 1–2*

**Ingredients**
- 1 whole fish (snapper, pomfret, seabass) or thick fillet
- 6 slices ginger, cut into fine strips
- 1 spring onion, shredded
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil (drizzle at the end)

**Method**
1. Score the fish, stuff ginger strips into the cuts and cavity.
2. Steam over high heat 8–12 min (10 min per 2.5 cm thickness).
3. Discard watery fish juice, top with fresh ginger + spring onion, drizzle soy + hot sesame oil.
