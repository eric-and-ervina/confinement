## 1. Sesame Oil Chicken (麻油鸡)

*Appears: days 8, 14, 15, 18, 22, 26*

**Ingredients**
- 500 g chicken (prefer 甘榜/camp chicken or bone-in thighs)
- 3 tbsp sesame oil (use roasted, not the pale cooking kind)
- 8–10 slices old ginger (老姜)
- 150 ml rice wine (米酒/黄酒) + water to cover
- 1 tbsp brown sugar (optional)

**Method**
1. Heat sesame oil in a wok over low–medium heat. Fry the ginger slices until golden and fragrant (5–7 min) — this is where the flavour lives.
2. Turn up heat, add chicken, sear until golden on all sides.
3. Pour in rice wine, then enough water to barely cover. Bring to a boil, reduce to a gentle simmer.
4. Simmer uncovered 25–30 min until tender and the wine smell has mellowed. Add sugar if you like.
5. Serve with rice or 面线.

**Trick:** simmer the wine at least 20–30 min after adding so the alcohol cooks off and only the fragrance remains.

> ⚠️ **Alcohol note (breastfeeding):** alcohol does **not** boost milk supply — and it passes into breastmilk — so treat the wine as flavour and tradition, not a supply tool. A 30-minute simmer still leaves ~30% of the alcohol; for peace of mind, simmer 60+ minutes, use low-alcohol confinement-brewed wine (米酒), or swap some of the wine for extra water/stock. **Practical rule:** cook the wine dishes in the evening, or plan feeds ~2 hours after eating them.

## 2. Green Papaya Fish Soup (青木瓜鱼汤) ⭐ Traditional Galactagogue

*Appears: days 15, 16, 18, 21, 26*

**Ingredients**
- 400 g fish (snakehead 生鱼, threadfin 午鱼, or batang 马鲛) — fillets + bones
- 1 semi-ripe green papaya, peeled, seeded, chunked
- 6–8 slices old ginger
- 1.5 L boiling water (this is the milky-broth secret)
- Salt to taste (light)

**Method**
1. Pat fish dry. Heat a little sesame oil in a pot, fry ginger until fragrant.
2. Add fish (bones first, then fillets) and fry until lightly golden.
3. Pour in **boiling** water — this instantly makes the broth milky white.
4. Add papaya chunks. Simmer 25–30 min until papaya is soft and translucent.
5. Season lightly, serve hot.

**Trick:** frying the fish + adding boiling water = the white colour. Never add cold water.

> 💡 **On the "lactation booster" label:** green papaya fish soup is a *traditional* galactagogue, but the evidence is anecdotal. What actually has evidence behind it: **oatmeal, adequate total fluids (~2–3 L/day of warm liquids), and frequent emptying of the breast** — more feeds/pumps = more supply. It's a lovely, nourishing soup regardless — serve it with pride, promise a bit less.

## 3. Herbal Black Chicken Soup (药材鸡汤)

*Appears: days 17, 19, 23, 24, 28*

**Ingredients**
- 1 black chicken (Silkie 乌鸡), chopped
- 1 confinement herbal pack (dang gui 当归, astragalus 黄芪, red dates 红枣, goji 枸杞, longan 龙眼) — or assemble your own
- 8 slices ginger
- 2 L water
- 1 tbsp rice wine (optional)

**Method**
1. Blanch the chicken in boiling water 2 min, drain (removes scum).
2. Rinse the dried herbs. Add everything to a pot or slow cooker.
3. Pour in 2 L water, bring to a boil, skim, then simmer 1.5–2 hours (or slow cooker 4 hours).
4. Season lightly. Drink the soup, eat the chicken.

**Shortcut:** buy pre-packed confinement herbal packs from Eu Yan Sang, Ma Kuang, or any Chinese medical hall — zero measuring needed.

> ⚠️ **Dang gui (当归) caution:** dang gui is a blood-moving herb. If lochia is still heavy — or bleeding restarts — pause the dang gui until it settles. Astragalus and red dates are safe throughout.

## 4. Yellow Wine Chicken (黄酒鸡)

*Appears: days 12, 20, 25*

**Ingredients**
- 500 g chicken, chopped
- 250 ml yellow wine (黄酒) or red yeast rice wine (红糟酒)
- 8 slices ginger
- 2 tbsp sesame oil
- 50 g black fungus (soaked, optional)

**Method**
1. Fry ginger in sesame oil until golden. Add chicken, sear well.
2. Add black fungus, then pour in the yellow wine and a splash of water.
3. Simmer 20–30 min so the wine mellows. Serve hot over rice.

> ⚠️ **Alcohol note (breastfeeding):** alcohol doesn't boost milk supply and passes into breastmilk — the wine is for flavour and tradition. Simmer at least 60 minutes (or use low-alcohol confinement wine) to keep the alcohol content minimal, and prefer cooking this in the evening or ~2 hours before a feed.

## 5. Ginger Millet Porridge (姜香小米粥)

*Breakfast staple, weeks 1–4*

**Ingredients**
- 100 g millet (小米) or rice
- 1 L water
- 5–6 slices ginger
- 6 red dates, pitted
- 1 egg (soft-boiled or poached, served on top)

**Method**
1. Rinse millet. Bring water + ginger + red dates to a boil.
2. Add millet, simmer 25–30 min stirring occasionally until thick and soft.
3. Top with an egg. Sweeten with a little brown sugar if she likes.

## 6. Red Date & Longan Tea (红枣龙眼茶)

*The bedside drink — make a big pot daily*

**Ingredients**
- 8 red dates, pitted, sliced
- 10 longan (龙眼) dried
- 2 slices ginger
- 1.5 L water
- Brown sugar to taste (optional)

**Method**
1. Simmer everything in a pot 30–40 min until the water turns amber and fragrant.
2. Strain into a thermos. Refill the bedside flask 2× daily. Warm, never cold.

## 8. Steamed Fish with Ginger (姜丝蒸鱼)

*Appears regularly in weeks 1–2*

**Ingredients**
- 1 whole fish (snapper, pomfret, seabass) or thick fillet
- 6 slices ginger, cut into fine strips
- 1 spring onion, shredded
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil (drizzle at the end)

**Method**
1. Score the fish, stuff ginger strips into the cuts and cavity.
2. Steam over high heat 8–12 min (10 min per 2.5 cm thickness).
3. Discard watery fish juice, top with fresh ginger + spring onion, drizzle soy + hot sesame oil.

# 🍚 Congee, Porridge & Oatmeal

## 9. Fish Congee (鱼粥)

*Appears: day 2*

**Ingredients**
- 100 g rice
- 1 L water
- 150 g threadfin (午鱼) fillet, or any white fish
- 6 slices ginger
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- Salt & white pepper to taste
- Spring onion, to finish

**Method**
1. Rinse rice. Bring water + ginger to a boil.
2. Add rice, simmer 30–40 min, stirring occasionally, until thick and soft.
3. Meanwhile, pan-fry the fish briefly in sesame oil until just cooked, then flake into chunks.
4. Fold the fish into the congee, season, simmer 5 more min.
5. Top with spring onion, serve hot.

**Trick:** fish cooked separately and folded in at the end keeps it sweet and tender, not mushy.

## 10. Black Chicken Congee with Goji (乌鸡枸杞粥)

*Appears: day 16*

**Ingredients**
- 100 g rice
- 200 g black chicken (Silkie), chopped small
- 1 tbsp goji berries
- 6 slices ginger
- 1.2 L water
- Salt to taste

**Method**
1. Rinse rice. Simmer black chicken + ginger in the water 30 min, skimming (this builds a rich base).

3. Add goji in the last 10 min so they plump but keep their colour.
4. Season lightly. The chicken is tender enough to eat straight from the congee.

## 11. Ginger Congee / Ginger Porridge with Egg (姜粥)

*Appears: days 6, 9, 13, 18, 25*

**Ingredients**
- 100 g rice
- 1 L water
- 8–10 slices old ginger
- 1 egg (soft-boiled or poached)
- Pinch of salt
- Few drops sesame oil

**Variation (day 3):** *Ginger red-date porridge* = add 6 pitted red dates with the rice for extra sweetness and blood-building. *Plain congee* (week-1 snack) = simply omit the ginger.

## 13. Red Date & Longan Oatmeal (红枣龙眼燕麦粥) ⭐ Lactation Breakfast

*Appears: days 4, 7, 10, 14, 15, 17, 19, 23, 26*

**Ingredients**
- 60 g rolled oats
- 500 ml water (or half water / half milk if she tolerates it)
- 6 red dates, pitted

- Brown sugar to taste

3. Sweeten lightly. Top with a soft-boiled egg if she wants extra protein.

**Trick:** oats + warm fluids are a proven lactation duo — in week 3 this is your milk-supply breakfast. Make a big batch of the date/longan base and reheat each morning.

## 14. Ginger Fried Rice with Chicken & Egg (姜炒饭)

*Appears: days 22, 28*

**Ingredients**
- 1 bowl cooked rice (day-old is best)
- 100 g shredded chicken (omit for the day-28 version)
- 1 egg
- 8 slices ginger, cut into fine matchsticks
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp light soy sauce
- Spring onion, chopped

**Method**
1. Heat sesame oil in a wok. Fry the ginger until golden and crisp; scoop half out and set aside.
2. Add chicken, toss 1 min, push to one side.
3. Crack in the egg, scramble, then mix everything together.
4. Add rice, break up clumps, stir-fry on high heat 2–3 min until piping hot.
5. Season with soy, toss in spring onion, top with the crisp ginger. Serve hot.

**Note:** this is the "graduating back to normal life" breakfast — save it for weeks 3–4.

# 🍲 Soups & Broths

## 15. Clear Chicken-Carrot Broth (鸡汤胡萝卜清汤)

*Appears: day 1*

**Ingredients**
- 300 g chicken (breast or thigh, bone-in)
- 1 carrot, chunked
- 6 slices ginger
- 1.2 L water
- Salt to taste

**Method**
1. Blanch the chicken in boiling water 2 min, drain (cleaner broth).
2. Add everything to a pot with the water. Bring to a boil, skim, simmer 45–60 min.
3. Season lightly. Serve hot with the tender chicken and carrot.

**Note:** this is the lightest soup of the plan — perfect for day 1 when digestion needs gentle handling.

## 16. Chicken Broth (清鸡汤) — the base

*Used in days 1 and 6, and the noodle bowls (recipes 26, 28)*

**Ingredients**
- 500 g chicken bones / carcass + 1 breast
- 8 slices ginger
- 2 L water
- Salt to taste

**Method**
1. Blanch the bones + breast 2–3 min, rinse well.
2. Simmer everything in 2 L water 1.5–2 hours (or slow cooker 4 hours), skimming.
3. Strain, season lightly. Shred the breast meat to use in noodle bowls.

**Trick:** batch-cook a big pot and freeze in meal-size portions — this broth powers mee sua, congee, and egg-drop soup all week.

*Appears: days 2, 4, 7, 9, 13, 20, 25 (the plan's "mild / white / clear fish soup")*

**Ingredients**
- 300 g fish bones + 100 g fillet (threadfin, snapper, or seabass)

**Trick:** same white-broth rule as the papaya fish soup — boiling water only, never cold.

# 🍜 Noodle Bowls

## 26. Mee Sua in Chicken Broth with Shredded Chicken & Egg (鸡汤面线)

*Appears: days 1, 7 (day 7 = same bowl with a poached egg)*

**Ingredients**
- 1 bundle mee sua (面线)
- 600 ml chicken broth (recipe 16)
- 100 g shredded chicken
- 1 egg
- 3 slices ginger
- Few drops sesame oil
- Spring onion, to finish

**Method**
1. Bring broth + ginger to a gentle simmer. Poach the egg in the broth 3 min, lift out.
2. Drop in the mee sua, cook 2–3 min until soft (it cooks fast — don't let it turn to mush).
3. Ladle noodles + broth into a bowl, top with shredded chicken, poached egg, sesame oil, spring onion. Serve very hot.

**Trick:** mee sua absorbs broth quickly — serve immediately, and keep a little extra hot broth to top up the bowl.

## 29. Rice Noodles with Beef & Greens in Broth (牛肉河粉汤)

*Appears: day 11*

**Ingredients**
- 150 g fresh rice noodles
- 100 g beef slices
- 1 handful greens (choy sum or spinach)
- 600 ml chicken or beef stock
- 3 slices ginger
- 1 tsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil

**Method**
1. Marinate beef with soy + sesame oil 10 min.
2. Bring stock + ginger to a simmer; blanch greens 30 sec, lift out.
3. Poach beef slices in the simmering stock 30–60 sec — they cook fast, keep them pink.
4. Warm noodles in the stock, assemble the bowl, ladle broth over. Serve hot.

**Trick:** never boil the beef — the residual heat of the broth finishes it. This is your week-2 iron boost.

# 🥚 Steamed Dishes

## 30. Steamed Egg Custard (蒸水蛋)

*Appears: day 2*

**Ingredients**
- 2 eggs
- 150 ml warm water or light broth (about 1.5× the egg volume)
- Pinch of salt
- 1 tsp light soy sauce
- Few drops sesame oil
- Spring onion, to finish

**Method**
1. Whisk eggs + warm water + salt until frothy.
2. Strain through a sieve into a shallow bowl (this is what makes it silky).
3. Cover the bowl with a plate or cling wrap — stops condensation from dimpling the surface.
4. Steam on medium-low heat 10–12 min until just set with a gentle wobble.
5. Drizzle soy + sesame oil, top with spring onion, serve warm.

**Trick:** low heat + covered + strained = restaurant-smooth custard. Over-steaming makes it spongy.

# 🐟 Pan-Fried Fish

## 34. Pan-Fried Pomfret with Ginger (姜煎鲳鱼)

*Appears: day 3*

**Ingredients**
- 1 pomfret (鲳鱼), cleaned and scored
- 6 slices ginger
- 1–2 tbsp sesame oil
- Pinch of salt
- Splash of rice wine (optional)

**Method**
1. Pat the fish completely dry, salt lightly.
2. Heat sesame oil, fry ginger until golden, push to one side.
3. Fry the fish 3–4 min per side over medium-high heat until golden and cooked through.
4. Splash wine around the pan if using, serve immediately with the crisp ginger on top.

> ⚠️ **Alcohol note:** a splash of wine here is a finishing deglaze that mostly evaporates, but if you want zero alcohol, just leave it out — the ginger-sesame crust carries the dish.

## 35. Pan-Seared Salmon (香煎三文鱼)

*Appears: days 4, 17*

**Ingredients**
- 1 salmon fillet, skin on
- 3–4 slices ginger
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- Pinch of salt
- Squeeze of lemon or splash of light soy sauce

**Method**

4. Rest 2 min, serve with the ginger + a squeeze of lemon (or light soy, if she's avoiding citrus).

**Note:** salmon's omega-3s are a lovely anti-inflammatory addition in weeks 3–4.

# 🥩 Stir-Fried Meat

## 38. Stir-Fried Beef with Ginger & Onion (姜葱炒牛肉)

*Appears: day 7*

**Ingredients**
- 150 g beef slices (sirloin or tenderloin)
- ½ onion, sliced
- 6 slices ginger
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp cornflour (to velvet the beef)
- Splash of water

**Method**
1. Velvet the beef: toss with soy + cornflour + 1 tbsp water, rest 10 min.
2. Heat sesame oil on high, fry ginger. Sear beef quickly until just browned, remove.
3. Stir-fry onion 1 min, return beef, toss 30 sec. Serve hot over rice.

**Trick:** high heat + quick sear = tender beef. Overcooking turns it tough.

# 🍖 Braises

## 42. Braised Pork Collar Slices with Ginger (姜焖猪颈肉)

## 43. Braised Pork Belly, Light Soy & Ginger (酱油姜焖五花肉)

*Appears: day 9*

**Ingredients**
- 300 g pork belly, cubed
- 8 slices ginger
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1.5 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- 500 ml water

**Method**
1. Sear the belly in a dry pan to render out some fat; drain the excess.
2. Fry ginger in sesame oil, return the belly.
3. Add soy + sugar + water, bring to a boil, simmer 1–1.5 hours until meltingly soft.
4. Reduce the sauce, serve over rice.

**Note:** "not too rich" — light soy + a little sugar only (no dark soy, no rock sugar, no deep-fried skin). The ginger keeps it warming.

## 66. Blanched Greens (白灼菜)

*Appears: days 1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 9, 13, 15, 20, 25, 27 (spinach, broccoli, kai lan, choy sum)*

**Ingredients**
- 1 bunch greens (spinach, broccoli, kai lan, or choy sum)
- 3 slices ginger
- 1 tsp light soy sauce
- Few drops sesame oil
- Splash of water

**Method**

**Note:** the gentlest way to eat greens — ideal for week 1 when digestion needs a soft landing. Drizzle with a little hot sesame-ginger oil for extra warmth.

# 🍠 Snacks

## 67. Black Sesame Paste (黑芝麻糊)

*Appears: days 8, 12, 20, 24 (breakfasts), plus week-2 snacks*

**Ingredients**
- 4 tbsp black sesame paste (黑芝麻糊 powder/paste)
- 500 ml warm water
- 2 tbsp brown sugar (to taste)
- 1 egg (optional, soft-boiled on top)

**Method**
1. Whisk the sesame paste with warm water until smooth — no lumps.
2. Warm gently 2–3 min, sweeten with brown sugar.
3. Top with a soft-boiled egg for protein if she wants. Serve warm.

**Trick:** jarred black sesame paste makes this a 2-minute breakfast. It's iron + calcium + healthy fats — a *traditional* milk-supply helper (anecdotal evidence, so enjoy it as a nourishing bonus rather than a guarantee).

## 68. Steamed Sweet Potato (蒸番薯)

*Week-2 snack*

**Ingredients**
- 1–2 sweet potatoes (orange or purple)

**Method**

**Note:** sweet, filling, and easy — the no-recipe recipe that deserves its place in the week-2 snack rotation alongside black sesame paste.
