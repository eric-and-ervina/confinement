# 🚫 TCM Herb Safety — Quick-Reference Card (fridge copy)

*Print this and stick it on the fridge next to the food plan's [`avoid card`](../meal/avoid-limit-card.md). Full detail in [`herb-guide.md`](herb-guide.md).*

> ⚕️ **Herbs are medicine.** Confirm every pack with a registered TCM physician, and tell your OB/midwife what she's taking. Nothing here replaces a doctor.

---

## ⏸️ PAUSE the herb if…

| Herb | Pause when |
|---|---|
| **当归 · 川芎 · 桃仁 · 益母草 · 红花** (blood-movers) | Lochia is **very heavy**, bleeding **restarts**, or she's on blood thinners |
| **人参 · 黄芪** | Fever, infection, a cold/flu, or an autoimmune flare — resume after |
| **肉桂 · 阿胶 · 干姜 · 龙眼 · lots of 老姜** (heaty) | Sore throat, mouth ulcers, constipation-with-heat, or a red-hot feeling — 热气 |
| **熟地黄** | Bloated or loose stools (it's cloying — add 陈皮 or reduce) |
| **炙甘草** | High blood pressure — keep the total ≤6 g/day and ask the doctor |
| **生化汤** | After **7 days**, if lochia is still bright red, or if bleeding is heavy — stop and ask |
| **Any herb** | Rash, itch, hives, vomiting, palpitations — stop immediately |

## ⛔ NEVER use (this month)

| Herb | Why |
|---|---|
| **麦芽 / barley malt** | **回乳 — it *reduces* milk.** Watch for it in "milk teas" |
| **薄荷 in quantity** | Can lower supply (small culinary amounts are fine) |
| **红花 / 桃仁 outside week 1** | Strong blood-movers — not for normal dishes |
| **麝香 · 牛黄 · 水蛭 · 三棱 · 莪术** | Too potent for home use; not while breastfeeding |
| **大黄 / 番泻叶 (laxatives)** | Pass into milk, cause cramps — use warm fruit + fluids |
| **人参 in week 1** | Too tonifying before lochia clears |

---

## ⚠️ Limit / handle with care

- **人参 (ginseng)** — only later, only if the physician advises; can be too hot and can raise BP.
- **黄芪** — generally safe, but check against any autoimmune condition or BP medication.
- **Wine (黄酒/米酒)** — flavour and tradition only; it does **not** boost milk. Alcohol passes into breastmilk — cook wine dishes in the evening or plan feeds ~2 h after.
- **Sugar (红糖)** — use as little as possible; a sweet dish most days is fine, a sweet-everything habit isn't.
- **Concentrated decoctions (生化汤, 益母草)** — treat as doses, not drinks. One pack a day, not "as much as you like."

---

## 🚨 Red flags — herbs stop, doctor comes first

Do not self-treat these with herbs. See [`../program/README.md`](../program/README.md) for the full list:

- **Heavy bleeding** — soaking a pad in an hour, or big clots
- **Fever ≥ 38 °C, chills, foul discharge** — infection
- **Calf pain/swelling/redness, or chest pain/breathlessness** — clot = emergency, call 995
- **Severe headache, blurred vision, sudden swelling** — possible pre-eclampsia
- **Wound opening / angry red and hot** — wound infection
- **Breast red, hot, wedge-shaped + flu feeling** — mastitis
- **Low mood turning dark, thoughts of harm** — postpartum crisis, seek help now

## ✅ Safe anchors (throughout the month)

- **党参 · 山药 · 莲子 · 茯苓 · 红枣 · 枸杞 · 陈皮 · 桑寄生 · 酸枣仁** — gentle, food-safe.
- **Plenty of warm fluids** (2–3 L/day) and **frequent feeding/pumping** — still the real milk drivers, herbs or no herbs.
- **Every herb cooked warm** — never cold decoctions, never raw.

*When in doubt: leave it out, and ask the TCM physician. That's not indecision — it's good care.* 🫶
