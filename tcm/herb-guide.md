# 🌿 The Herb Guide — Formulary, DIY Packs & Rules of Use

Every herb with **measured grams**, its TCM role, and the hard rule for when to pause it. Buy loose at a Chinese medical hall and ask them to weigh and bag it, or copy these numbers into your own shopping note. **Have a registered TCM physician review the packs first** (see the warning in [`README.md`](README.md)).

> **Buying tip (Singapore):** Eu Yan Sang, Ma Kuang, Hockhua, and most neighbourhood 药材店 will weigh loose herbs to these amounts and seal them in daily packs. Ask for **硫磺-free / 无硫** (unsulphured) where offered — better taste and safer. Tell them it's for **产后 / confinement** and they'll recognise every formula below.

---

## 🔑 How to cook any herb pack (the one method)

1. **Rinse** the herbs quickly in cold water; drain. (Skip rinsing 枸杞, 阿胶.)
2. Tie loose herbs in a **muslin bag** (or a clean cotton cloth), or leave loose and scoop the big slices out before serving.
3. Add the bag + **2 L water** to the pressure cooker (or a pot). If the recipe has meat, add the blanched meat too.
4. **Pressure cooker:** select **煲汤 / soup**, ~25–35 min. **Stove:** bring to a boil, then simmer 45–60 min.
5. Add **枸杞 in the last 5–10 min** so it keeps its colour and fragrance.
6. **Remove the herb bag, drink the soup, eat the meat.** Never eat the 当归/川芎/肉桂 themselves — they've given what they have.
7. **Decoctions** (生化汤) are different — see the pack table below.

> **One pack = one pot = 1–2 servings.** Don't reuse a bag — the medicine is spent.

## 📋 The master formulary

### Blood herbs 血药
| Herb | Amount per pot | TCM role | Pause if… |
|---|---|---|---|
| **当归 Dang gui** | 10–15 g | 补血活血 — builds and moves blood; the queen of 月子 | Lochia heavy, or bleeding restarts |
| **川芎 Chuan xiong** | 6–9 g | 活血行气 — moves qi, eases after-pains, headache | Heavy bleeding / not in week 1 unless in 生化汤 |
| **白芍 Bai shao** | 10 g | 养血柔肝 — softens cramps, calms, nourishes blood | Rarely — generally gentle |
| **熟地黄 Shu di huang** | 12–15 g | 补血滋阴 — deep blood and yin tonic | 消化不良/loose stools (it's cloying); 热气 with 口干 heat signs |
| **阿胶 E jiao** | 6–10 g | 补血止血 — rich blood tonic, dissolves in warm soup | Very 热气 constitution; congestion; heavy lochia |
| **益母草 Yi mu cao** | 10–15 g | 活血祛瘀 — uterus and lochia; a gentle 生化汤 alternative | Heavy bleeding; not long-term |

## 🧺 The 7 DIY packs (measured)

**1. 生化汤 Sheng Hua Tang — the week-1 decoction** *(days 1–7, typically 5–7 packs)*
| Herb | Amount |
|---|---|
| 当归 Dang gui | 15–24 g |
| 川芎 Chuan xiong | 6–9 g |
| 桃仁 Tao ren (crushed) | 6 g |
| 炮姜 Pao jiang (or 干姜) | 1.5–2 g |
| 炙甘草 Zhi gan cao | 2 g |
*(Guiding: 15–30 ml 黄酒/米酒 can be added at the end; traditionally 童便, now commonly omitted.)*
**Method:** decoct (煎) — simmer in 1.2 L water ~40 min → strain → drink, **twice** from one pack (re-simmer the same herbs with 0.8 L a second time). **Not a soup** — a medicine. **Pause if lochia is heavy.** Confirm dosing with the TCM physician.

**2. 四物汤 Si Wu Tang — blood base** *(weeks 2–4, 1–2×/week)*
熟地黄 15 g · 当归 10 g · 白芍 10 g · 川芎 6 g → simmer with 500 g chicken or lean pork + 2 L water (煲汤).

**3. 八珍汤 Ba Zhen Tang — blood + qi** *(weeks 2–3, 1–2×/week)*
四物 (above) **+** 党参 10 g · 白术 10 g · 茯苓 10 g · 炙甘草 6 g. Add 生姜 3 slices + 红枣 4 for the guiding herbs. Great with black chicken.

**4. 十全大补汤 Shi Quan Da Bu Tang — the full tonic** *(weeks 3–4, 1–2×/week)*
八珍汤 **+** 黄芪 10 g · 肉桂 3 g (or 桂枝 5 g). The richest soup in the month — serve with 陈皮 3 g so it digests.

**5. 通草鱼汤 Tong Cao Fish Soup — milk-opening** *(weeks 3–4, 2–3×/week)*
通草 3–6 g **+** green papaya + 午鱼/生鱼/鲫鱼 + 老姜. Add the bag with the soup; **take it out before serving.** (This is the upgradable version of the food plan's signature soup.)

**6. 杜仲强腰汤 Du Zhong Strong-Back Soup** *(weeks 3–4, 2×/week)*
杜仲 10–15 g · 续断 10 g · 桑寄生 10 g · 红枣 4 · 老姜 5 slices, with 猪骨/龙骨 (pork bones — **no trotters, no innards**) + 2 L water. For the aching back and loosened ligaments.

**7. 益母草饮 Yi Mu Cao Decoction — lochia support** *(week 1, only if flow is sluggish)*
益母草 10–15 g decocted in 800 ml water ~25 min; drink once. Use **instead of** 生化汤 that day, not alongside, unless the physician says otherwise.

## ⛔ Never use — the confinement avoid-list

| Herb | Why |
|---|---|
| **麦芽 / 大麦芽 (barley malt)** | **回乳 — suppresses milk.** Common in "milk-regulating" teas that do the opposite of what you want |
| **薄荷 (peppermint) in quantity** | Can reduce supply; small culinary amounts in toothpaste/tea are fine |
| **红花 (safflower), 桃仁 after week 1** | Strong blood-movers — fine inside 生化汤 under supervision, but not to be sprinkled into normal dishes |
| **麝香, 牛黄, 水蛭, 三棱/莪术** | Potent/contraindicated herbs — not home-use, not while breastfeeding |
| **大黄 / 番泻叶 (laxatives)** | Pass into milk, cause cramps; use warm fruit + fluids instead |
| **人参 (ginseng) in week 1** | Too tonifying before lochia clears; can be too hot for some constitutions — only later and only if the physician advises |

*If in doubt, leave it out and ask the TCM physician. See [`safety-card.md`](safety-card.md) for the fridge version.*
