# 🌿 TCM Herbal Confinement Plan — 中药月子餐 (28 days)

A **herb-forward** companion to the food-first plan in [`../meal/`](../meal/README.md). Same 28 days, same Singapore home kitchen — but this time the 药材 are the point. Every formula here is given as **DIY measured amounts** (grams), because that's what a Chinese medical hall sells you.

Where the food plan says *"serve it with pride, promise a bit less,"* this plan says *"here is the 生化汤, here is the gram of 川芎."* It's the version your mother-in-law recognises.

> ⚕️ **Read this before you buy a single herb.**
> This is a well-researched, traditional *cooking-and-tonic* guide — **not a prescription, and not a substitute for a licensed TCM physician (中医师) or your wife's doctor.** Chinese herbs are real medicine: they interact with drugs, they affect bleeding and blood pressure, and several are **not safe while breastfeeding or when lochia is heavy.** The formulas below are the *standard classical amounts* — but the right herb, dose, and duration for **her** body, delivery, and medical history is a ten-minute consultation. **Have a registered TCM physician confirm the pack before week 1**, and tell your OB/midwife if you're adding herbs (especially 当归, 人参, 黄芪, 红花). When bleeding, fever, or any red-flag symptom appears, herbs stop and the doctor comes first.
>
> **If you can only do one thing:** buy the pre-packed confinement herbal packs from a registered hall and let them dose it for you. The DIY tables are here because you asked for them — not because self-prescribing is wise.

## The TCM logic in one line

**化瘀 → 补气血 → 通乳 → 固本.** First clear the old (lochia), then build the new (blood and qi), then open the milk, then strengthen the bones, back, and constitution.

### The four phases

| Phase | Days | TCM purpose | Lead herbs | Herbs to **hold** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — 化瘀生新** | 1–7 | Expel lochia, protect digestion, no tonifying until the old blood clears | **生化汤** (当归 川芎 桃仁 炮姜 炙甘草), 益母草 if flow is sluggish | No 人参, no 十全大补, no heavy 熟地 — tonifying too early can prolong lochia |
| **2 — 气血双补** | 8–14 | Rebuild blood and qi, wake the spleen (digestion), steady the mood | **四物汤**, **八珍汤**, 党参, 山药, 莲子, 红枣, 龙眼 | Hold 红花 and strong blood-movers; keep 桃仁 out after week 1 |
| **3 — 通乳进补** | 15–21 | Establish milk (通乳) + deep tonification | **通草** (in fish soups), **八珍 / 十全大补**, 杜仲 starts | Avoid 麦芽 (barley malt) — it *reduces* milk. Avoid 薄荷 in quantity |
| **4 — 固本调理** | 22–28 | Consolidate: bones, back, ligaments, constitution; step toward normal | **十全大补**, **杜仲 + 续断**, **阿胶** (day 28), richer tonic soups | Keep rich tonics moderate; watch 热气 (heatiness) |

> 💡 **C-section note:** many TCM physicians hold 生化汤 or modify it for a surgical delivery, and start tonics later. **Also add 生鱼 (snakehead) soup** 4–5× in weeks 1–2 for wound healing — the food plan already does this. Confirm with the TCM physician.

> 💡 **Bottle-feeding note:** if she isn't breastfeeding, delete the 通草/通乳 phase — everything else still applies.

## The four rules of herbal confinement

1. **Order matters more than strength.** 化瘀 before 补. A strong tonic in week 1 while lochia is still flowing is the classic beginner mistake — it can make bleeding heavier and last longer.
2. **Know your pause herbs.** Blood-moving herbs (当归, 川芎, 桃仁, 益母草, 红花) pause if lochia is very heavy or bleeding restarts. Heaty herbs (干姜, 肉桂, 人参, 龙眼, 老姜) ease off if she gets a sore throat or mouth ulcers.
3. **The spleen must cooperate.** Every tonic still has to be *digested* — so soups stay light, 党参/山药/莲子/陈皮 carry the heavy formulas, and you never serve a bitter decoction and a greasy meal together.
4. **The doctor outranks the herbalist, always.** Infection, fever, heavy bleeding, high blood pressure, thyroid, clots — those go to the doctor. Herbs resume after.
