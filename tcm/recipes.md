# 👩‍🍳 The Herbal Recipes — Measured, Decoctions & Tonic Soups

Every recipe here uses the **pressure cooker / pot method** from [`../meal/recipes.md`](../meal/recipes.md) — you're only changing the ingredients. Amounts are per **1 pot = 1–2 servings** unless noted. Rinse herbs quickly; bag loose ones; **remove the bag before serving**; add 枸杞 last.

> ⚕️ Amounts are the classical standards. Confirm with a TCM physician before day 1.

---

# 🧪 Decoctions 煎剂 — medicine, not soup

## 1. 生化汤 Sheng Hua Tang *(week 1, days 1–7)*

**Per pack (one day):** 当归 15–24 g · 川芎 6–9 g · 桃仁 6 g (crushed) · 炮姜 1.5–2 g · 炙甘草 2 g

1. Put all herbs in a pot (not the pressure cooker — a gentle decoction is better here). Add **1.2 L water**. Soak 20 min.
2. Bring to a boil, then **simmer 40 min** with the lid ajar. Strain into a bowl — this is the **first decoction (头煎)**.
3. **Second decoction (二煎):** return the same herbs to the pot with **0.8 L water**, simmer 30 min, strain. Combine both, split into 2 cups.
4. Drink warm — one cup morning, one cup evening, **30 min after food**. Optional: stir 1 tsp 米酒 into the second cup.
5. **One pack per day, 5–7 days.** Pause if lochia is heavy; ask the physician if you're unsure.

> ⚠️ 生化汤 is a **medicine**, not a regular soup. Don't double the herb amount, don't take it cold, and don't extend past 7 days without advice.

## 2. 益母草饮 Yi Mu Cao Decoction *(only if lochia is sluggish, week 1)*

益母草 10–15 g · 红糖 1 tsp (optional)
Simmer the herb in 800 ml water 25 min, strain, drink warm once. **Use instead of 生化汤** that day, not alongside.

# 🍲 Tonic Soups 补汤 — the heart of the plan

## 3. 四物汤 Si Wu Tang *(weeks 2–4, 1–2×/week)*

**Herbs:** 熟地黄 15 g · 当归 10 g · 白芍 10 g · 川芎 6 g
**With:** 500 g chicken or lean pork · 老姜 6 slices · 红枣 4 · 2 L water

1. Blanch meat 2 min, drain. Rinse herbs, bag the loose ones.
2. Add everything + 2 L water to the pressure cooker; **煲汤 / soup**, ~30 min.
3. Remove the bag, season lightly, drink the soup and eat the meat.
> Black chicken (乌鸡) is the classic pairing for the richest version.

## 4. 八珍汤 Ba Zhen Tang *(weeks 2–4 — blood AND qi)*

**Herbs:** 熟地黄 15 g · 当归 10 g · 白芍 10 g · 川芎 6 g · 党参 10 g · 白术 10 g · 茯苓 10 g · 炙甘草 6 g
**With:** 1 black chicken · 老姜 6 slices · 红枣 4 · 2 L water

1. Blanch chicken 2 min. Rinse herbs; bag them.
2. Pressure cooker **煲汤 / soup** ~30–35 min. Add 枸杞 1 tbsp in the last 5 min.
3. Remove the bag, season lightly, serve hot. Tastes sweet and mild — the easiest big tonic to drink.

## 5. 十全大补汤 Shi Quan Da Bu Tang *(weeks 3–4 — the full tonic)*

**Herbs:** everything in 八珍 (above) **+** 黄芪 10 g · 肉桂 3 g · **陈皮 3 g** (so it digests easily)
**With:** 1 black chicken or 500 g pork ribs · 老姜 6 slices · 红枣 5 · 2 L water

1. Blanch meat; rinse herbs and bag them (keep 陈皮 in too).
2. Pressure cooker **煲汤 / soup** ~35 min. 枸杞 1 tbsp last 5 min.
3. Remove the bag, season, serve. The richest soup of the month — **don't serve it with a fried or greasy meal.**

## 6. 通草木瓜生鱼汤 Tong Cao Fish Soup *(weeks 3–4 — milk-opening)*

**Herbs:** 通草 3–6 g
**With:** 400 g 生鱼/午鱼 (or 鲫鱼) · 1 semi-ripe green papaya · 老姜 8 slices · 1.5 L **boiling** water

1. Pressure cooker **开盖煮 (open-lid)**: heat a little 麻油, fry the ginger, then the fish bones until golden.
2. Pour in **boiling** water (milky broth rule), add papaya + the 通草 (bagged).
3. Close, **煲汤 / soup** ~20–25 min. Remove the 通草 bag. Season lightly, serve hot.
> **Take the 通草 out.** It's done its job. This is the food plan's signature soup, upgraded.

## 7. 杜仲强腰汤 Du Zhong Strong-Back Soup *(weeks 3–4, 2×/week)*

**Herbs:** 杜仲 10–15 g · 续断 10 g · 桑寄生 10 g
**With:** 400 g pork bones (龙骨 / 脊骨 — no trotters) · 红枣 4 · 老姜 5 slices · 2 L water

1. Blanch bones 2–3 min, rinse. Rinse herbs, bag them.
2. Pressure cooker **煲汤 / soup** ~30–35 min.
3. Remove the bag, season lightly, drink the soup and eat the meat. For the aching back and loose ligaments.

# 🍚 Herbal Congees & Breakfasts

## 8. 党参山药莲子粥 Dang Shen Congee *(weeks 2, days 8 & 13)*

党参 10 g · 山药 15 g (dried) · 莲子 15 g · 100 g rice · 6 red dates · 1 L water · 1 egg to top

1. Rinse the herbs; bag the 党参.
2. Add herbs + red dates + water + rice to the pressure cooker; **粥 / porridge**.
3. Remove the bag, season lightly, top with a soft-boiled egg. Gentle, sweet, spleen-friendly — the perfect week-2 breakfast.

## 9. 八宝粥 Eight-Treasure Congee *(day 10 breakfast)*

红小豆 20 g · 莲子 15 g · 龙眼肉 10 g · 红枣 6 · 枸杞 1 tbsp · 山药 15 g · 糯米/rice 60 g · water 1 L
Rinse, add all except 枸杞 to the pressure cooker; **粥 / porridge**; add 枸杞 last 5 min. Sweet enough without sugar — keep it that way if you can.

## 10. 红枣龙眼茶 Red Date & Longan Tea *(daily, all month)*

红枣 8 (pitted) · 龙眼肉 10 g · 老姜 2 slices · 1.5 L water
Simmer 30–40 min (open-lid or hob pot) until amber. Thermos it. **Ease off if 热气.** This is the bedside drink — warm, never cold.

## 11. 红枣黄芪茶 Red Date & Astragalus Tea *(days 8–14 afternoons)*

黄芪 15 g · 红枣 6 · 枸杞 1 tbsp
Simmer 黄芪 + 红枣 25 min, add 枸杞 last 5 min. For tiredness in the blood-building and tonic weeks. Skip on fever days.

# ✨ Finishing touches

## 12. 阿胶烊化 How to use E Jiao *(day 28, or when advised)*

阿胶 is a **solid** blood tonic — never boil it. Break 6–10 g into a bowl with 2 tbsp warm water; sit the bowl in hot water and stir until it melts (*烊化*), then stir the liquid into a bowl of hot 八珍/十全大补 soup just before drinking. **Skip if she's very 热气 or congested.**

## 13. 当归蒸鸡 Steamed Chicken with Dang Gui *(alternative to the food plan's 枸杞蒸鸡)*

1 chicken thigh/breast · 当归 4 g (2 thin slices) · 枸杞 1 tbsp · 老姜 6 slices · 1 tsp 麻油 · 1 tsp light soy
Rub the chicken; lay 当归 slices, ginger and 枸杞 on top; steam (rack + boiling water) 12–18 min. A weekly herbal upgrade — **pause the 当归 if lochia is heavy.**

## 14. 桑寄生杜仲蛋 Sang Ji Sheng Eggs *(weeks 3–4 backup)*

桑寄生 10 g · 杜仲 10 g · 2 eggs · 800 ml water
Simmer herbs 20 min; add boiled-in-shell eggs (cracked) and simmer 10 min more. Eat the eggs, sip the liquid. A light back-support snack when a full soup is too much.
