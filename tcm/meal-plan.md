# 📅 The 28-Day TCM Plan — 中药月子

The food-first plan with a **herbal backbone**. Meals reuse the cooking method in [`../meal/recipes.md`](../meal/recipes.md); the herbal recipes live in [`recipes.md`](recipes.md) and every amount is in [`herb-guide.md`](herb-guide.md).

**Read the day like this:** `Herbal element` is the TCM intervention for that day — a decoction, a tonic soup, or a milk-opening soup. Meals stay warm, small, and frequent.

> ⚕️ Confirm all packs and the 生化汤 with a registered TCM physician before day 1. Pause any blood-moving herb if lochia is very heavy or bleeding restarts.

---

## Phase 1 — 化瘀生新 (Days 1–7): clear the old

**Purpose:** expel lochia, protect digestion, **no tonifying** yet. 生化汤 decoction daily; food is light and warm. Add 生鱼 (snakehead) soup in week 1 if C-section.

| Day | Breakfast | Lunch | Dinner | Herbal element |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Ginger millet porridge + egg | Rice, steamed snapper, blanched spinach, clear chicken-carrot broth | Mee sua in chicken broth + egg | **生化汤 #1** (1 pack) |
| **2** | Fish congee | Rice, steamed egg custard, choy sum, tofu soup | Rice, braised pork collar w/ ginger, broccoli, mild fish soup | **生化汤 #2** · 益母草 if flow sluggish |
| **3** | Ginger red-date porridge + egg | Rice, air-fried pomfret, stir-fried lettuce, pork rib-corn soup | Rice noodles w/ chicken & bok choy | **生化汤 #3** |
| **4** | Oatmeal w/ red dates + egg | Rice, steamed chicken w/ goji, kai lan, egg-drop soup | Rice, air-fried salmon, sweet potato leaves, white fish soup | **生化汤 #4** |
| **5** | Millet porridge + egg | Rice, steamed seabass, stir-fried zucchini, clear pork rib soup | Sesame-oil mee sua w/ chicken | **生化汤 #5** |
| **6** | Ginger porridge + egg | Rice, lean pork w/ mushrooms & ginger, blanched spinach, tofu soup | Steamed fish, stir-fried cabbage, chicken broth | **生化汤 #6** |
| **7** | Oatmeal + red dates | Rice, beef w/ ginger & onion, blanched choy sum, fish soup | Mee sua w/ poached egg + chicken | **生化汤 #7 — last one; lochia should be settling** |

**Teas:** warm red date tea. **No tonic soups this week.** If bleeding is still bright and heavy on day 7, stop 生化汤 and ask the physician.

## Phase 2 — 气血双补 (Days 8–14): build the new

**Purpose:** rebuild blood and qi, strengthen the spleen, warm her back up. 四物 / 八珍 start; 党参 + 山药 + 莲子 settle the digestion. Sesame oil chicken and wine dishes return.

| Day | Breakfast | Lunch | Dinner | Herbal element |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **8** | Black sesame paste + egg | Rice, sesame oil chicken, broccoli, tofu soup | Rice, steamed threadfin, black fungus + snow peas, peanut pork rib soup | **党参山药莲子 congee** — gentle spleen tonic |
| **9** | Ginger congee + egg | Rice, braised pork belly (light soy + ginger), okra, fish soup | Rice, steamed chicken w/ ginger, blanched spinach, red date & chicken soup | **四物汤** (chicken) |
| **10** | Eight-treasure congee (see recipes) | Rice, steamed pomfret, cauliflower, pork collar & carrot soup | Rice, sesame oil pork slices, kai lan | **八珍汤** (black chicken) |
| **11** | Red-date porridge + egg | Rice, air-fried cod, nai bai, egg & tomato soup | Rice noodles w/ beef + greens | **红枣黄芪 tea** (daily sipper) |
| **12** | Black sesame paste | Rice, yellow wine chicken, zucchini, fish tofu soup | Rice, steamed seabass, braised lotus root, pork rib soup | **四物汤** (lean pork) |
| **13** | Ginger porridge + egg | Rice, steamed chicken thigh w/ goji, sweet potato leaves, clear fish soup | Rice, pork collar w/ black fungus, blanched broccoli | **党参山药莲子 congee** |
| **14** | Oatmeal + egg | Rice, sesame oil chicken, spinach, peanut pork rib soup | Steamed fish, stir-fried mixed veg, red date chicken soup | **八珍汤** (black chicken) — blood #2 |

**Teas:** 红枣龙眼茶 daily; 红枣黄芪茶 in the afternoons if she's tired. **Watch 热气** (sore throat, ulcers) — that's your cue to dial back the ginger, longan, and 肉桂.

## Phase 3 — 通乳进补 (Days 15–21): open the milk, go deep

**Purpose:** establish supply with 通草, and tonify deeply. This is the richest week — 八珍 and 十全大补, 杜仲 for the back, 阿胶 for blood. Feed/pump often; fluids 2–3 L/day.

| **16** | Black chicken congee w/ goji | Rice, steamed threadfin, asparagus, green papaya fish soup | Rice, braised pork collar w/ chestnuts, cabbage | **杜仲强腰汤** |
| **17** | Oatmeal + red dates | Rice, **八珍 black chicken soup**, broccoli | Rice, air-fried salmon, nai bai | **八珍汤** (primary) |
| **18** | Ginger porridge + egg | Rice, steamed seabass, spinach, **通草 papaya fish soup** | Rice, sesame oil chicken, black fungus | **通草鱼汤** ② |
| **19** | Oatmeal + longan | Rice, **十全大补汤** w/ black chicken, choy sum | Rice, steamed pomfret w/ ginger, okra | **十全大补汤** + **杜仲** |

**Teas:** 红枣龙眼茶 (supply + warmth), 通草 can also go into the daily drinking soup. **Reminder:** 通草 bag out before serving. **Never** use 麦芽 tea — it suppresses milk.

## Phase 4 — 固本调理 (Days 22–28): consolidate

**Purpose:** strengthen the bones, back, and constitution; a last deep tonic before normal life. 十全大补 + 杜仲 + 阿胶 to finish. Diet broadens toward balanced family meals.

| Day | Breakfast | Lunch | Dinner | Herbal element |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **22** | Ginger fried rice w/ chicken + egg | Rice, sesame oil chicken, kai lan, peanut pork rib soup | Steamed fish w/ fermented black beans, mixed veg | **杜仲强腰汤** |
| **23** | Oatmeal + egg | Rice, braised pork belly w/ chestnuts, spinach, **十全大补汤** | Rice, air-fried threadfin, zucchini | **十全大补汤** (primary) |
| **24** | Black sesame paste | Rice, **八珍汤**, broccoli | Rice, steamed seabass, braised lotus root | **杜仲强腰汤** |
| **25** | Ginger congee + egg | Rice, yellow wine chicken, sweet potato leaves, **通草 fish soup** | Rice, braised beef brisket w/ ginger, choy sum | **通草鱼汤** (final) |
| **26** | Oatmeal + red dates | Rice, **十全大补汤**, asparagus | Rice, sesame oil chicken, nai bai | **十全大补汤** |
| **27** | Red-date porridge + egg | Rice, steamed pomfret, spinach, pork rib-corn soup | Rice, braised pork collar w/ mushrooms, broccoli | **杜仲 + 续断 soup** |
| **28** | Ginger fried rice w/ egg | Rice, **八珍 black chicken soup**, kai lan | Rice, steamed fish, mixed veg — celebrate 🎉 | **阿胶烊化** in the soup · 十全大补 goodbye |

**After day 28:** drop to 1–2 tonic soups a week for a month if she wants to keep going — 四物/八珍 alternating, 杜仲 for the back. The TCM physician can advise a longer 调理.

| Herb missing | Use instead |
|---|---|
| 生化汤 pack | 益母草 decoction 10–15 g, once daily (week 1 only) |
